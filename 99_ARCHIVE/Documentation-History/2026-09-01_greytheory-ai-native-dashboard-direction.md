# GreyTheory AI-Native Dashboard Direction — Documentation History

On 2026-09-01 the operator reopened the visual direction after the Research
Ledger typography pass. The live preview audit found that the record-first shell
still did not serve the intended learner journey and that desktop and 390-pixel
states clipped content.

Repository truth now distinguishes:

- the existing Research Ledger as a partial evidence-view baseline;
- the modern AI-native learner dashboard as a product direction awaiting
  operator selection;
- implemented deterministic learning, mastery, application, and read-model
  foundations from the graphical Today/Learn/Practise/Prove experiences that
  are still planned; and
- the Windows-local learner pilot from the later Ubuntu isolated-worker and VPS
  transition.

The change adds Mermaid diagrams for the learner loop and deployment transition
and does not change operating posture or target capability.
