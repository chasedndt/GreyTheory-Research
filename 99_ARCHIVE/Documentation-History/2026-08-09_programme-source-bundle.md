## Historical change

This pass moved real-programme compilation from roadmap intent to an executable, offline source-bundle capability. The first proof packages GitLab's public HackerOne scope export with bounded programme and platform-policy extracts, then binds the normalized authority record to those sources through hashes, precedence, citations, and deterministic scope derivation.

It matters because GreyTheory can no longer treat a programme as one unstructured text blob or silently normalize away source rows. Source change invalidates review, unresolved human decisions block compilation, and saved public evidence grants no live authority.
