- Date: 2026-08-25
- Runtime: Codex / Axiom-Codex
- Status: PARTIAL and VERIFIED offline

## Historical change

The UI-neutral workbench boundary gained its first research mutation path. A
future selected shell can now record an unproven hypothesis, pause for explicit
human scope review, and persist one bounded experiment plan. Revisions and
atomic store ownership make stale or half-written planning state fail closed.

This did not add execution. Authority is derived from existing persisted state,
the scope step records human judgement without widening the underlying
contract, and accepted results explicitly remain non-executing.
