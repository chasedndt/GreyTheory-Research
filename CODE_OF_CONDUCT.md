# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be straight with people. Argue with the work, not the person. Assume the other party is competent and acting in good faith until they give you a concrete reason not to.

## Expected

- Direct, technical, specific feedback.
- Disagreement backed by reasoning or evidence.
- Saying "I was wrong" plainly and moving on.
- Respecting that maintainers may decline a contribution without it being a judgement on the contributor.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments.
- Discrimination on any basis.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

Because of what this project is, two more:

- **Do not post details of live vulnerabilities** in issues, pull requests or discussions. Use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Do not post evidence, target names, or programme-confidential material.** If an example is needed, use the `.test` fixtures.

## Reporting

Raise conduct concerns privately with the maintainer. Reports will be handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, decline contributions, or block accounts where behaviour is inconsistent with the above.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commits — and when representing the project publicly.
