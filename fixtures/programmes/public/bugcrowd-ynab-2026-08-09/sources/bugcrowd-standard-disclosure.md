## Relevant platform defaults

- The programme owner maintains the programme brief, and programme-specific terms take precedence over the platform defaults.
- Testing is limited to systems listed in the programme brief's Targets section; all other systems are out of scope unless the brief states otherwise.
- Researchers normally create test accounts rather than using third-party accounts.
- Submissions and submission communications stay within Bugcrowd or official support channels.
- Actions that affect target integrity or availability are prohibited; automated activity must stop if performance degrades.
- Proof material must not be placed on publicly accessible services.
- Bugcrowd disclosure policies apply to submissions, with the programme brief selecting the applicable disclosure mode.

This extract is supporting platform context. It does not override the YNAB brief and grants no authority.
