Authority facts retained for compilation:

1. Core ineligible findings apply across HackerOne bug bounty and vulnerability-disclosure programmes unless a programme explicitly changes them.
2. Findings without realistic security impact are generally ineligible.
3. Optional hardening and missing-best-practice reports are generally ineligible.
4. Hazardous testing must never be attempted unless explicitly authorised.
5. Hazardous testing includes availability-impacting traffic, social engineering, noisy user/admin actions, and physical-facility attacks.
