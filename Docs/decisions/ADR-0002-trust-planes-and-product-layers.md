# ADR-0002 — Trust Planes and Product Layers

- Date: 2026-08-09
- Status: ACCEPTED

## Decision

Retain Authority, Signal, and Judgement as ranked trust planes. Add product layers for programme intelligence, research workspaces, asset graphs, knowledge, hypotheses/experiments, execution, signals, evidence/reporting, outcomes/learning, and workbench/integrations.

## Consequences

Planes answer who may trust or authorise something. Layers answer what the product lets a researcher do. No layer may bypass or weaken a plane.
